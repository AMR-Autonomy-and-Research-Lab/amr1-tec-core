Improvements:

Braking System:
-Braking System should be on both sides of the car. 
-Especially in the front (70% of braking power).
-Braking zylinder should have a housing. 
-Only the brake line should be outside of it.
-The braking system should be redundant.

Steering System:
-Steering should be a fixed system. 
-The chain can get off and the car is without steering afterwards. 
-If it´s only possible with the chain it should atleast be in a housing.
-No starting position for the steering. 
-The system has no information about the steering position.

General safety aspects:
-No lights or reflectors.
-No crush zone.
-No damping system. Big problematic for safety and component wear.
-Depending on the capacity of the amr and especially the use in hot region, the use of a cooling system
 for the batteries and the power unit should be considered.

To ensure building the same amr again, the use of rivets or pins is necessary.
